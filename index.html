Our Story

Ammu Organics is a traditional food business from Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, built around the idea of bringing naturally prepared and traditionally made food products to everyday families.

We value traditional methods, carefully selected ingredients and direct relationships with our customers. From a question about a product to an order update, we want it to be simple to speak with us.
